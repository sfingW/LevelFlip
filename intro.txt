<architecture_hybrid_model>
LevelFlip bypasses prohibitive OPRA real-time data fees by utilizing a hybrid data ingestion pipeline:
1.  **Static State (The Anchor):** Open Interest ($OI$) is established overnight by the OCC. We fetch this baseline options matrix (Strikes, $OI$, Volume, IV) at 09:30 AM ET via `yfinance`.
2.  **Dynamic State (The Catalyst):** We stream sub-second tick data for the underlying asset price ($S$) via a free WebSocket feed (e.g., `StockSocket`).
3.  **Synthesis:** As the spot price updates in real-time, the backend recalculates the Black-Scholes partial derivatives in memory, yielding a live-reacting Gamma profile without requiring a $10k/month data license.
</architecture_hybrid_model>

<tech_stack_definitions>
*   **Backend (Engine):** Python, FastAPI, `uvicorn`.
*   **Quant Core:** `numpy`, `scipy.stats` (Strictly vectorized operations; sequential `for` loops across option chains are forbidden).
*   **State Management:** `cachetools.TTLCache` (2-second TTL). Zero external databases (No Postgres, No Redis).
*   **Frontend (Client):** Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS.
*   **Visualization:** 
    *   `lightweight-charts` (TradingView canvas for price action and level overlays).
    *   `recharts` (Horizontal bar profiles for net GEX distribution).
</tech_stack_definitions>

<mathematical_parameters>
The quantitative engine must isolate three structural boundaries:
1.  **Call Wall:** $\max( (OI_{calls} \times 0.3) + (Volume_{calls} \times 0.7) )$
2.  **Put Wall:** $\max( (OI_{puts} \times 0.3) + (Volume_{puts} \times 0.7) )$
3.  **LevelFlip (Zero Gamma):** The strike where the aggregate market net gamma transitions from positive (volatility dampening) to negative (volatility accelerating).
